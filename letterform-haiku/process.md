# Letterform Q

*From a hand-drawn sketch to a digital letterform*

![My letterform Q](Q.svg)

## Hand-drawn Starting Point

First of all, I drew the uppercase Q by hand. The heavy geometric shape became the basis for the digital version.

## Scan and Digital Correction

I mirrored the form digitally to correct its direction, then refined the outline while keeping the original weight.

## Testing Three Cuts

I tested three typeface cuts: Regular, Bold and Condensed.

## Final Decision

I chose the Regular cut because it kept the character of my original drawing. The Bold version felt too heavy, while the Condensed version changed the proportions too much.
